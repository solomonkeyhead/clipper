# Studio (Control Center)

Scope: every page of the Studio web app. Mode: Operate. Audience: the owner now, any short-video maker later.
Task: get from footage to posted video with the least work; always see the next thing to do.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md, D120 (one highlight colour only for the next action, money in its own colour), all copy and features kept, docs/REDESIGN_NOTES.md open problems solved.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The work is a wall of vertical posters, your clips and Shorts, in rows by status; the one in focus grows and says what to do with it. Refuses the stock dark dashboard: metric tiles, cards on cards, pill chips, kanban.

OWN-WORLD: Blue-black ground (light mode: cool daylight grey), flat surfaces separated by space, not boxes; 9:16 artwork is the only colour; amber marks the one next action (focus ring and the primary button), green is money only. Sofia Sans Condensed, tall and upper case, for titles and row heads; Sofia Sans for everything else; metadata as plain text joined by dots, never pills.

STORY: Open the Studio, see the best thing to post already in focus with its button, rows of what else is waiting below, money in one quiet line at the top.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with the nav and paid money; the focused poster at 9:16 left with title, score, length, campaign, estimate, Post button; rows titled by status below.

FORM: streaming catalog wall (challenger pop-culture-shelf-streaming-title-card-wall), chosen over my list; seed 2c981bd6. Signature move: focus expansion, the focused tile lifts and shows its actions while its row dims; J/K moves focus.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
