Refund policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Clipper is sold by {{operator_name}}. Our order process is run by Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle takes your payment and sends your refund.

Subscriptions (Starter, Pro, Agency)

  • Within 14 days of your first payment for a plan, you get a full refund, whatever you used. Ask once per account.
  • After 14 days we do not refund part of a period that has already started. You can still cancel at any time, and you keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Renewals: cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. If a renewal charged you and you had no use of it, write to us the same week and we will look at it.

Packs

You can have a refund for a pack within 14 days of buying it if none of it has been used. Once part of a pack is used, we do not refund the rest.

If you live in the EU or the UK

As a consumer you have the right to withdraw from a purchase within 14 days without giving a reason. Clipper starts working as soon as you use it, so if you have already used part of the service, the refund is reduced in proportion to what you used. Paddle handles these requests for us under its checkout terms, and your rights under the law of your country stay as they are.

If something went wrong on our side

If Clipper failed to deliver what you paid for, for example a build that never finished and used your allowance, write to us. We will give the allowance back or refund you, whichever you prefer.

How to ask

  1. Open the receipt Paddle emailed you and follow the link to request a refund, or go to paddle.net and look up your order.
  2. Or email {{contact_email}} with the email address on your account and the date of the payment.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. The time it takes to show up depends on your bank or card, usually 5 to 10 business days.
