How it works

  1. Put a video inUpload a file, or paste a public Google Drive or Dropbox link. Add the campaign brief.
  2. Get ranked clipsClipper finds the strongest moments, cuts them to vertical, and shows a reason for each pick. It checks each clip against the brief's rules and writes captions, a title and hashtags.
  3. Change what you like, then postMove the start or end, rewrite the hook or the captions, change the framing. Download the clip and post it yourself.

What you get

  • Clipping. Long videos in, ranked vertical clips out, for paid clipping campaigns. Each clip is checked against the brief before you post it.
  • Every part can be changed by hand. Start, end, hook, captions and framing. The same controls are on every plan, the free one included. Plans differ only in how much you can make, the watermark, how long clips are kept, how many accounts and seats you get, and how fast the queue is.

  • Stats. Connect your TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts to see how your posts perform and match each post to the clip it came from. Stats sync starts on each platform once that platform approves Clipper.

Not here yet: Clipper does not post for you (you download your clips and post them), and it does not make a voice for you.

Questions

What counts as a minute?

Minutes of source video you put in. A one-hour video uses 60 minutes. A video is billed at least 10 minutes, so a 3-minute video uses 10.

What does a Short include?

One Short is one first build of a Create video plus 5 redraws. A redraw is New drawing, Change drawing, or a drawing replaced by New pictures or Redraw all. Every further 5 redraws use one more Short. Rebuilds that redraw nothing are free.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel on the Billing page. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for, and it does not renew.

What about refunds?

If you ask within 14 days of your first payment for a plan, you get your money back. The refund policy has the full rules, including the right to withdraw for buyers in the EU and UK.

Which platforms does it work with?

Clips are plain vertical video files, so you can post them anywhere. Stats sync works with TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts that you connect, starting once each platform approves Clipper. Clipper does not post for you yet.

Is my footage used to train AI?

No. Clipper sends parts of your video and text to Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini) and Groq (speech to text) through paid API accounts. Under those terms the providers do not use what we send to train their models. The privacy policy lists every provider and what it receives.

Do I need the rights to my videos?

Yes. You must own the video or have permission to use it for clipping. The terms say what that means.

Does the AI get it right every time?

No. Picks, captions and rule checks can be wrong. That is why each pick shows its reason and each part can be changed. Check every clip before you post it.
