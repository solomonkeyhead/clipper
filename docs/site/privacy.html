Clipper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Clipper is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps the owner make short video clips and keep track of how their own posts perform. This page explains what Clipper does with information it receives from Google APIs.

What Clipper accesses

When the owner connects their YouTube channel, Clipper asks Google for read-only access (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly) to read:

  • the connected channel's name and handle;
  • the channel's uploaded videos: title, description, publish date and length;
  • each video's public counts (views, likes, comments) and its analytics (average view duration and shares).

Clipper cannot upload, change or delete anything on YouTube, and does not read anything outside the connected channel.

How it is used

The information is used only to show the owner how their own posts are doing, inside Clipper, and to match each post to the clip it came from.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on the owner's computer, in Clipper's data folder. Clipper has no server; nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. The sign-in token is stored in that same folder.

Sharing

Clipper does not sell, share or transfer this information to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

Clipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Disconnect the channel on Clipper's Accounts page, which deletes the stored sign-in, and remove Clipper's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Statistics already saved can be deleted with Clipper's data folder.
