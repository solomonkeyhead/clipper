Clipper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Clipper is a tool that runs on its user's own computer. It helps them make short video clips and keep track of how their own posts perform. This page explains what Clipper does with information it receives from Google, TikTok and Instagram when a user connects an account.

What Clipper accesses

When the owner connects their YouTube channel, Clipper asks Google for read-only access (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly) to read:

  • the connected channel's name and handle;
  • the channel's uploaded videos: title, description, publish date and length;
  • each video's public counts (views, likes, comments) and its analytics (average view duration and shares).

Clipper cannot upload, change or delete anything on YouTube, and does not read anything outside the connected channel.

The same applies to TikTok (user.info.basic, video.list: the account's name and its videos' public counts) and Instagram (instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_manage_insights: the account's name and its Reels' counts and insights). All access is read-only.

How it is used

The information is used only to show the owner how their own posts are doing, inside Clipper, and to match each post to the clip it came from.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on the user's computer, in Clipper's data folder, including the sign-in tokens.

The sign-in service. To log in, the user is sent through a small sign-in service run by the developer. It passes the user to Google, TikTok or Instagram, receives the sign-in tokens, and hands them straight to the user's own computer. It does not store them, and keeps no accounts, no statistics and no logs of them. It sees the tokens for a moment while it does this, and again when a token is renewed. Nothing else is sent to it.

Sharing

Clipper does not sell, share or transfer this information to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

Clipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Disconnect the account on Clipper's Accounts page, which deletes the stored sign-in, and remove Clipper's access in the platform's settings (Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions; Instagram: Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites). Statistics already saved can be deleted with Clipper's data folder.
