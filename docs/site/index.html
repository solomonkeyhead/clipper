Clipper

Clipper is a personal tool for making short vertical clips from long videos for paid clipping campaigns, and for keeping track of how the owner's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts posts perform. It runs on the owner's computer; it has no accounts, no server and no ads.

When a YouTube channel is connected, Clipper reads that channel's video statistics (read-only) and keeps them on the owner's computer.
